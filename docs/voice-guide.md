# Cielo Verde — Voice & Tone Guide

## Voice
First person. Conversational. No performative positivity. Write like you're texting a friend who's genuinely interested, not pitching to a stranger.

## What it sounds like
- Honest about where things are, not where you want them to be
- Specific — real dates, real people, real places
- Uncertain when uncertain. "I don't know yet" is fine.
- Short. One thought at a time.

## What to avoid
- Sanctuary, journey, sacred, intentional, aligned
- Stock language about nature, transformation, or retreats
- Emojis used for emphasis
- Hashtag walls
- Anything that sounds like it was written by a brand

## What every post should have
- Something true
- Something specific
- A reason to care that isn't "support my dream"

---

## By Channel

### Website
Full story, full context. The permanent record. Everything else links back here. Longer sentences are fine because people opted in by clicking.

### Instagram
One image, one idea. Caption is 2-4 sentences max. No setup, no conclusion — drop into the middle of it. End with a fact, not a call to action. Link in bio does the work.

### Facebook
Slightly longer than IG because your network wants more context. Still no more than a short paragraph. Works best when it feels like something you'd actually post on your personal page — because that's where the trust already is. Good for sharing IG posts with a sentence of context added.

### Captions that work (examples of the voice)
- "Daniel cleared this Saturday. Before and after. This is what $50 looks like on the ground."
- "He pooled his own money with a friend to buy the motoguadaña. It stays on the land."
- "Selling a guitar to fund this. If you're curious: discovercieloverde.com"
- "Primary cloud forest at 1,600m. Never cleared. No signal. A family kept it alive for 25 years."

### What doesn't work
- "We are so excited to share..."
- "This land has taught us so much"
- "Join us on this journey"
- Tagging brands or accounts hoping for a repost
- Posting without a photo
